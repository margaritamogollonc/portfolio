How I work

I start by understanding the product and the buyer: what problem it solves, how it fits into existing workflows, and what objections decision-makers already have. Then I map that insight to a clear structure — problem, impact, solution, proof — so every piece has a job to do in the funnel. I use AI as a collaborator for research and ideation, but keep humans in charge of strategy, narrative, and final quality.

Article opening (excerpt)

Most IT leaders assume their most secure systems are the ones that handle sensitive data. They're usually wrong.

In many organizations, the biggest risk doesn't come from careless people — it comes from software built for efficiency first and security second. Tools that were never designed for today's threat landscape end up holding your most critical data together with duct tape.

For a SaaS platform, that gap shows up as silent churn, stalled deals, and long sales cycles with security teams. In this article, we break down where those gaps typically appear — and how tightening your product's security story can turn a liability into a revenue driver.
